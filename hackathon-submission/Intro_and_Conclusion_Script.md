## Recording tips

- **Framing:** sit side by side, both clearly in frame, camera at eye level. A phone camera on a stand works best.
- **Light:** face a window or lamp. Never sit with the light behind you.
- **Sound:** quiet room with the fan/AC off, phone about an arm's length away.
- **Same setup:** record Phase 1 and Phase 3 in the same place, so they match.
- **Takes:** record 2–3 takes of each phase and keep the best one.
- **Pace:** speak slowly and clearly, and pause for half a second between speakers.
- **Talk to the camera,** not to the script. Keep the script just below the camera.
